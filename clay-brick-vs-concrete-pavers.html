Clay Brick vs Concrete Pavers: Durability and Appearance

Both genuine kiln-fired clay paving bricks and high-density concrete pavers build durable outdoor surfaces when laid over a sound base, yet they weather differently over decades.

FactorKiln-fired clay paving brickPrecast concrete pavers
Color over timeNatural mineral clay color runs throughout and resists UV fadingPigmented concrete gradually softens over years; sealers refresh tone
Shape & pattern varietyClassic rectangular modular formatsWide range of large-format slabs, tumbled cobbles, and interlocking shapes
Dimensional consistencySlight natural kiln variation requires careful string-line checksTightly molded with built-in spacer bars for uniform joint width
Cold-climate durabilityMake sure pavers—not wall bricks—are graded SX for freeze-thaw cyclesHigh-PSI architectural pavers perform well; avoid harsh de-icing chemicals on fresh units
