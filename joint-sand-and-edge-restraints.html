Paver Edge Restraints and Polymeric Joint Sand Notes

Interlocking pavers transfer weight from stone to stone through sand-filled joints—a mechanism that collapses immediately if the outer row is free to drift outward into lawn soil.

Finishing details contractors should never skip

  • Rigid edge restraint on all unbordered sides: Heavy-duty spiked paver edging (pinned into the compacted aggregate base, never just into loose bedding sand) or a troweled concrete haunch locks perimeter stones tight.
  • Dry pavers before polymeric sand: Sweep polymeric sand only across completely dry stones, compact with a pad-equipped plate compactor so sand settles deep into joints, and blow every trace of polymer dust off paver faces before misting.
  • Controlled misting: Over-flooding washes polymer binder out of joints; follow the exact bag watering instructions and keep surfaces clean so haze never cures onto stone texture.
